The Reader-Writer Portfolio

This is the last project of 5th grade. It has two parts. Part 1 is the comparison essay you finished in Week 31. Part 2 is your letter to a future 5th grader, with everything that goes with it: your Reader’s Portrait, five Book Postcards, the letter, a Word Gift, a P.S. and an envelope. It all goes into one booklet.

What to do
  1. Paste in each part from your lessons, one section at a time.
  2. Check that every quote is copied exactly from your book, with its chapter.
  3. Tap Make my booklet, save it as a PDF, and upload the PDF in Canvas.

Your work saves on this computer as you type. Your copied work from Lessons 31.4 and 32.1–32.4 has every part you need.
