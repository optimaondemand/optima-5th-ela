Grammar: Complex Sentences for Cause and Effect
3 points
Correction 1 pt
1Combine these two short sentences into ONE complex sentence that shows cause and effect. Use a subordinating conjunction such as because, since, when, or although.
Johnny’s hand was badly burned. He could no longer work as a silversmith.
Identification 1 pt
2Read the complex sentence below. Which part is the cause clause?
Because Rab believed in Johnny’s story, he found a lawyer willing to defend him for free.
  • Ahe found a lawyer
  • BBecause Rab believed in Johnny’s story
  • Cwilling to defend him for free
  • DRab
Sentence Application 1 pt
3Write your own complex cause-and-effect sentence about your OWN story’s main character. Use a subordinating conjunction (because, since, when, although, after, before).
Pattern: [Subordinating word] + [cause], [effect]. Or: [effect] + [subordinating word] + [cause].

Morphology & Vocabulary: Roots narr / fic
3 points
Word-Part Breakdown 1 pt
4Break down the word narrator into its parts. What does the root narr mean, and what does the whole word mean?
Hint: narr = “to tell”; fic = “to make or form”
Meaning from Context 1 pt
5Read the sentence. What does fictional most likely mean here?
“Even though my fictional character never really lived, I researched real events from 1773 so her story would feel true to history.”
  • ABased entirely on a true story with no changes
  • BInvented or made up, rather than a real historical person
  • CWritten only in the form of a letter or diary
  • DTold by more than one narrator at the same time
Use It 1 pt
6Choose one word: narrate, narrator, fiction, or fictional. Write a sentence that shows you understand its meaning. Try to connect it to your own story if you can.

Reading & Craft: A New Excerpt
2 points
“The three backs were bent once more over the ledgers. The quill pens scratched. He heard the gritting of sand as they blotted their pages.”
— Esther Forbes, Johnny Tremain, Chapter IV, “The Rising Eye”
Identify a Craft Move 2 pts
9What craft move is Forbes using in this excerpt — sensory detail, dialogue, or choosing a form? Name the craft move, then explain how it helps you picture the counting house and feel what Johnny feels as he waits.

Writing: Revise for Voice and Sensory Detail
2 points
Revise the Sentence 2 pts
10This sentence is flat — it tells us what happened but gives us nothing to see, hear, or feel. Rewrite it with stronger voice and at least one sensory detail.
The boy walked into the office and talked to the man.
Ask yourself: What did the boy hear, see, or smell? What did his voice sound like? What specific verb could replace “walked” or “talked”?
