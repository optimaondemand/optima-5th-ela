Morphology & Vocabulary: Roots scrib / script
3 points
Word-Part Breakdown 1 pt
4Break the word inscription into its word parts and explain what each part means.
Hint: in- (a prefix meaning “into”) + script (a root meaning “to write”) + -ion (a suffix meaning “the act of”)
Choose Meaning From Context 1 pt
5Read the sentence below. What does manuscript mean in this context?
The old silversmith kept a manuscript of family records, written by hand, tucked inside his shop's strongbox.
  • Aa document written by hand
  • Ba printed newspaper
  • Ca locked box for silver
  • Da spoken promise

Reading & Craft: Spot the Move
2 points
Craft Move Identification 1 pt
9Read the new excerpt below from Johnny Tremain, Chapter I. Which craft move is Forbes using here?
Johnny was already in his leather breeches, pulling on his coarse shirt, tucking in the tails. He was a rather skinny boy, neither large nor small for fourteen. He had a thin, sleep-flushed face, light eyes, a wry mouth, and fair, lank hair.
— Esther Forbes, Johnny Tremain, Chapter I
  • ADialogue between two characters
  • BSensory, physical detail that helps the reader picture Johnny
  • CA comparison using “like” or “as”
  • DA historical date or event
Explain 1 pt
10Explain how this craft move helps you picture Johnny. Use at least one specific detail from the excerpt.

Writing: Mimic the Model
2 points
RWM Mimic Sentence 2 pts
11Study the model sentence below, then write your own sentence using the same frame.
Model Sentence
“All right,” said Johnny blandly, “just so you’re up.”
Frame: ‘___,’ said [Name] ___ly, ‘___.’
Notice how Forbes places the dialogue tag in the middle of the sentence and uses an adverb ending in -ly to show exactly how the character speaks.
