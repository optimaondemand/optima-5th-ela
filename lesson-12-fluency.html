🎙️Week 12 · Fluency

This week Nat discovered hundreds of errors in Moore’s Navigator — and decided to fix them all. Today’s passage captures Nat at the moment of that decision. Read it as if you understand exactly what is at stake: not just calculations, but sailors’ lives.

📜 Week 12 Fluency Passage
The errors were not small. They were not occasional. They were everywhere — in every table, on every page, woven through the book that sailors trusted with their lives. Nat sat at his desk aboard the Henry, the almanac open before him, and he counted. One hundred errors. Then two hundred. Then more. He did not put the book down. He picked up his pen. If the book was wrong, someone would have to make it right. And if no one else would do it, he would.
— Jean Lee Latham, Carry On, Mr. Bowditch, Chapter 13 (adapted for fluency)
1
Read for Smoothness
The first paragraph builds. Let each sentence land before moving to the next. The final two sentences are the turning point — pause slightly before “And if no one else would do it, he would.”
2
Read for Expression
This is a quiet, determined moment. Nat is not angry — he is resolved. Let your voice carry that quiet certainty. The final sentence — “he would” — should feel inevitable, not dramatic.
