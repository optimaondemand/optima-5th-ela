📜 Week 14 Fluency Passage — Chapter 24
The fog closed around the ship like a curtain drawn by invisible hands. No shore. No lighthouse. No landmark in any direction — only grey. The crew stood at the rails and said nothing. They had stopped asking questions an hour ago.

Only Nat moved. He checked his figures at the chart table one last time, folded his papers, and walked to the bow. He stood there looking into the grey. He could not see through it.

He did not need to.

He knew where they were. He knew where Salem Harbor lay — its mouth, its depth, its bearing from their current position. The fog had changed nothing. The mathematics was exact. The ship moved forward, and the grey thinned, and the dock appeared exactly where Nat had said it would be.
— Jean Lee Latham, Carry On, Mr. Bowditch, Chapter 24 (adapted for fluency)
🔭 Your Final Fluency Path
1
Read for Phrasing
The two short sentences in the middle — “He could not see through it.” and “He did not need to.” — carry the whole meaning of the book. Each one gets its own breath. Do not run them together.
2
Read for Expression
Tone: quiet certainty. Not fear, not triumph. Nat knows. The crew doesn’t, yet. Read the passage from the position of someone who already knows how this ends — calm, still, precise.
