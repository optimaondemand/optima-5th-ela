Week 31 Fluency: Your Favorite Passage
🎙️Week 31 · Fluency

This week you don’t read a passage your teacher assigned. You choose one. Look back over the year’s reading — five novels, dozens of chapters — and find the passage that stuck with you most. It might be funny, moving, dramatic, or just beautifully written. Whatever made you think: this is what great writing can do.

📜 Your Choice — Best Passage from This Year

Select a passage from one of the year’s novels.

You will read it aloud for your teacher.

Possibilities: any scene from The Witch of Blackbird Pond, Johnny Tremain, Carry On, Mr. Bowditch, or By the Great Horn Spoon!

Your novel • Your choice • Your year
📋 How to Choose Your Passage
🦁Courage: A moment when a character does something that cost them something — and did it anyway.
😂Comedy: A scene that made you laugh — and showed you something true at the same time.
💔Emotion: A scene that surprised you with how much you cared.
✍️Writing: A passage where the writing itself is so good you want to read it twice.
🏆Significance: The scene that best captures what this novel — or this year — was about.
🔍 Your Fluency Path
1
Practice for Smoothness
Read at a natural pace. The passage should feel like it flows, not like you’re sounding out words. If you stumble, return to the sentence start.
2
Practice for Expression
This is your performance. Think about every character’s voice. Think about the pace — where to slow down, where to speed up, where to pause. Let the passage show why you chose it.
3
Record and Submit
Record your reading. Before the passage, say: your name, the title of the novel, and one sentence about why you chose this passage. Then read. Upload to Canvas.
📤 How to Submit

Record yourself saying: (1) your name, (2) the novel title, (3) one sentence about why you chose this passage. Then read the passage with your best accuracy, smoothness, and expression. Upload to Canvas.
