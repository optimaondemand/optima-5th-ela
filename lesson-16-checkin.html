Grammar: Sentence Variety
3 points
Identification 1 pt
1Read these two sentences. Which one is the short sentence used for emphasis?

Correction 1 pt
2This passage is choppy — every sentence is short and starts the same way. Rewrite it with sentence variety, combining some ideas into longer sentences:

Reading Comprehension: Central Idea & Author’s Purpose
3 points
“In 1830, the United States government pressed the Choctaw Nation to sign the Treaty of Dancing Rabbit Creek. Under its terms, the Choctaw ceded about eleven million acres of homeland in Mississippi in exchange for land far to the west, in what is now Oklahoma. The removal did not happen all at once. Between 1831 and 1833, Choctaw families traveled west in three separate waves, facing a brutal winter and, later, a cholera epidemic along the way. Roughly fifteen thousand Choctaw people made the journey, and about twenty-five hundred did not survive it. A smaller number of Choctaw families — five to six thousand people — chose to remain in Mississippi despite the hardships they knew they would face there.”
— This Week’s Reading (new excerpt)
Central Idea 1 pt
9What is the central idea of this passage?
  • ACholera was the only danger Choctaw families faced during removal.
  • BThe Treaty of Dancing Rabbit Creek forced a difficult, costly removal that some Choctaw families experienced and others avoided by staying behind.
  • COklahoma was a better homeland than Mississippi had been.
Cite a Detail 1 pt
10Cite one specific detail from the passage that supports the central idea you chose above.
Author’s Purpose 1 pt
11Why do you think the author chose to include both the number of people who made the journey west and the number who chose to stay in Mississippi?
