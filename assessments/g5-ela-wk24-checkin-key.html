13 Writing · Written (teacher-graded) · 1 pt

The novel begins, “Maybe you have never read a book written by a ghost before. I am a ghost.” It ends with Pushmataha saying, “Chi pisa lachike. I will see you again.” In 2–3 sentences, explain why this is a fitting ending for a story told by a ghost.

Key: Choctaws never say good-bye; the dead stay with the living. Isaac, a ghost, has stayed with his family the whole book, and the ending promises that the people and the story go on.
