Reading passage (Chapter 10)

“Son,” he said, “you cannot keep your eyes on the bloody footprints you have left behind you. You must keep your eyes on where you are going.”
I took a deep breath. I nodded. From that moment on, even though my feet burned, I refused to look behind me. I looked where I was going and forgot about the pain.

Tim Tingle, How I Became a Ghost, Chapter 10
