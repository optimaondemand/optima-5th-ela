# G5 ELA — Carry On, Mr. Bowditch accuracy audit (weeks 9–14)

Audited Oct 29, 2026, against the novel itself (Houghton Mifflin, 1955). The text came from two sources: a fresh OCR of all 24 chapter PDFs in `optima-5th-ela/planning docs/carry on mr bowditch novel/`, and the Internet Archive lending copy borrowed on Bethany's account (archive.org/details/carryonmrbowditc00jean). Nothing in the repo was edited.

## Bottom line
- **37 files checked.** These are the 24 chapter lessons, 6 check-ins, 6 fluency pages and the unit writing workshop, plus the Bowditch mentions in 31.2, 32.3 and the 32 check-in.
- **About 170 findings:** 105 ERROR, 45 LIKELY, 5 HISTORY, 15 CHECK. Every CHECK has now been resolved (see below).
- Only the **writing workshop** came back clean.
- **Three patterns cause most of the problems:**
  1. **Invented "quotes" credited to Latham.** These include nearly every banner/hero/epigraph quote in weeks 10–14, **all six fluency passages** (weeks 9–14), and check-in reading passages built on those lines. These are graded items.
  2. **Chapters described from their titles, not their content.** Many lessons put events in the wrong chapter or describe things that never happen:
     - Moore's errors are found in ch 13 (one error), not ch 7. The "eight thousand" count comes in ch 20.
     - Nat starts teaching sailors in ch 12, not ch 8.
     - Nat is clerk and second mate on the *Henry*. He is first supercargo on the *Astrea*.
     - Ch 14 "Nineteen Guns" means the *Astrea*'s nineteen guns, not a salute.
     - Ch 17 is set ashore (the husking bee and the marriage), not on a lunar at sea.
     - Ch 18 is a convoy escort and Elizabeth's death, not a rescue.
     - Ch 21's "choice" between sealing and navigation doesn't exist.
     - The Sumatra voyage is ch 23, not ch 22.
  3. **Vocabulary credited "from Chapters X–Y" that never appears in the book.** Examples: terrestrial, erroneous, hazardous, intersect, submarine, interval, transfer.
- **Wrong speaker:** Nat says "Two plus two is four. Always…" to Hab in ch 3. Lesson 9.3, the week 9 check-in and the week 9 fluency page credit it to Dr. Stearns.
