**1. Grammar · Comparison Sentence** (1 pt) Which comparison sentence is written correctly?
- Although Kit Tyler hides her friendship with Hannah. Jack Flagg tells the captain the truth.
- Although Kit Tyler hides her friendship with Hannah, Jack Flagg tells the captain the truth. ✓
- Kit Tyler hides her friendship with Hannah, Jack Flagg tells the captain the truth.
- Although Kit Tyler hides her friendship with Hannah; Jack Flagg tells the captain the truth.

**2. Grammar · Combine with While** (1 pt) Combine these two sentences into one comparison sentence that begins with While.
> Kit Tyler stands trial in Wethersfield. Johnny Tremain carries messages in Boston.
- Key: While Kit Tyler stands trial in Wethersfield, Johnny Tremain carries messages in Boston. (While opens the sentence; a comma ends the while clause.)

**3. Grammar · Semicolon** (1 pt) Which sentence uses a semicolon correctly?
- Isaac loses his home; and Jack leaves his by choice.
- Isaac loses; his home and Jack leaves his by choice.
- Isaac loses his home; Jack leaves his by choice. ✓
- Isaac loses his home, Jack; leaves his by choice.
