The Witch of Blackbird Pond
🎙️ Week 3 · Fluency

This week you have been reading about Kit's growing friendship with Hannah Tupper and her secret visits to the cottage by Blackbird Pond. Today you will practice reading a passage from Chapter 10 with accuracy, smoothness, and expression. Great readers don't just say the words — they bring the story to life with their voice.

This Week's Fluency Passage
The little hut with its tipsy chimney was like something out of a tale. Two cats perched on the rail of a sagging little porch. The door stood open. Inside, Kit could make out the glow of a fire and the flicker of a candle flame. An old woman sat hunched on a settle by the hearth, a gray cat curled in her lap. She turned toward the sound of Kit's footsteps, and Kit saw a face so old and weathered that it might have been carved out of oak. But the eyes that met hers were as bright and knowing as a child's.
— Elizabeth George Speare, The Witch of Blackbird Pond, Chapter 10 (adapted)
🗺️ Your Fluency Path
1
Read for Smoothness
Read the passage again. This time, keep your voice flowing. Don't stop between words — let the sentences move like a river, pausing only at commas and periods.
2
Read for Expression
Read it one more time. Now bring the scene to life! Use a hushed, wondering tone for the description of the cottage. Slow down at the final sentence — let the reader feel the contrast between Hannah's weathered face and her bright, knowing eyes.
3
Record and Submit
When you feel ready, record yourself reading the passage with your best accuracy, smoothness, and expression. Upload your recording to Canvas.
✅ Fluency Self-Check
🎯 Did I say each word correctly?
🌊 Did my reading sound smooth — like flowing water, not choppy waves?
🎭 Did my voice show the feeling — the quiet mystery of the cottage, and the surprise of Hannah's bright eyes?
