A Ghost’s-Eye View

You finished How I Became a Ghost. Isaac told you the whole story, and from Chapter 18 on he told it as a ghost. Now make a four-page booklet that shows how the book works: who tells it, what it is about, who is in it, and how its words make pictures.

What to do
  1. Fill in the four pages, one at a time. Keep your book open.
  2. Use at least 3 details or quotes from the book, each with its chapter number.
  3. Tap Make my booklet, save it as a PDF, and upload the PDF in Canvas.

Your work saves on this computer as you type. This project is about the novel. Keep real history out of it unless you say where the fact comes from.

Page 1 · Who tells the story?

Isaac tells this story himself, and he tells you on the first page that he becomes a ghost. That choice changes what you, the reader, get to see and know.

Think about where he can go and what he can see after Chapter 18.

Page 2 · Theme tracker

A theme is a big idea the whole book keeps coming back to. Choose one, then track it through three moments: one from the beginning, one from the middle, and one from the end.

Page 3 · A character and a picture in words

First, show what one character is like. Then find one place where Tim Tingle uses a figure of speech.

Look for a simile (it uses like or as), a metaphor, or a thing that acts like a person.

Page 4 · Reflection

Use formal style: no slang and no contractions. Name the book and the author.
