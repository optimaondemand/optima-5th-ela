🔍 Editing Checklist: CUPS

🔠 Capitalization

Every sentence starts with a capital letter. Proper nouns (character names, place names) are capitalized too.

✍️ Usage

Subjects and verbs agree. Verb tense stays consistent (don’t slip between past and present).

❓ Punctuation

Sentences end with the right punctuation. Dialogue has quotation marks and correct end punctuation inside them.

📖 Spelling

Watch suffix spelling changes on narrative verbs: drop-e (hope → hoping), double-consonant (run → running), y-to-i (carry → carried).

the soldier stoped and lisened. → The soldier stopped and listened. (capitalization + double-consonant spelling)

She said "wait for me → She said, “Wait for me!” (comma before quote, capital inside quote, closing punctuation and quotation mark)
